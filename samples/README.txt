Drop your sample pack in this folder (any folder layout: Kicks/, Snares/, Loops/ ...).
AI Beat Studio sorts sounds by their names: kick, snare, clap, hat, open hat, crash, 808/bass, loops, vocals, FX, perc.
Loops with the tempo in the name (e.g. "Dark Groove 140bpm.wav") are stretched to your song's BPM.
Then press "Rescan" in the Samples section of the app.
Sample packs are not committed to git (check each pack's licence before sharing).
